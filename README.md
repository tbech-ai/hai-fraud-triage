## Pilot test (25/11/2026)
- One informal test with one person (my roommate, not a fraud analyst)
- Calibrated mode, feedback on, all 40 cases
- Participant's own description of their approach: "trusted high percentages"
- Followed the AI on 25/30 correct cases (83%) and 6/10 wrong cases (60%)
- Followed the AI on every case shown at 70% or above (30/30) and on 1 of 10 at 60%
- Their accuracy was 29/40; the AI's was 30/40
- Follow rate stayed similar early vs late (80% vs 75%) despite per-case feedback
- Limits: one participant, one condition, no comparison with overstated mode yet

## Second pilot test (25/11/2026)
- One informal test with a different person (my other roommate, not a fraud analyst)
- Overstated mode, feedback on, all 40 cases
- Followed the AI on 29/30 correct cases (97%) and 9/10 wrong cases (90%)
- Overrode only twice, both at 75% shown confidence (accuracy 30/40, equal to the AI's)
- Compared with the calibrated pilot: both participants seemed to follow the AI above roughly 70% shown confidence; overstated mode raised the lowest level from 60% to 75%
- Limits: two different participants, one run each, no statistics; a pattern to explore, not a finding
